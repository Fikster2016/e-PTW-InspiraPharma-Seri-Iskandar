SECTION 2 — ON-SITE & WORK COMPLETION
To be completed on the day of work commencement and upon work completion.
Toolbox Talk (TBT) Confirmation
General Site Safety Rules  —  For reference during TBT briefing. Supervisor to read aloud and confirm understanding with all workers before work commences.
  1. Always wear the required PPE such as safety helmet, safety shoes, and other protective equipment according to the work activity.
  2. Use a safety harness with proper anchorage when working at height.
  3. Keep the work area clean, tidy, and free from hazards at all times.
  4. Do not bring vehicles into the work area without approval.
  5. Smoking, vaping, gambling, fighting, horseplay, and sleeping in the work area are strictly prohibited.
  6. Follow all Safe Work Procedures (SWP), SOPs, safety signages, barricades, and Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) requirements.
  1. Do not stand or work under suspended loads or lifting activities.
  2. Obtain the required Permit to Work (PTW) before carrying out hot work, confined space entry, work at height, or other high-risk activities.
  3. Dispose of waste properly and follow site environmental, hygiene, and GMP requirements at all times.
  4. Report all unsafe conditions, near misses, incidents, injuries, spills, and damaged equipment immediately to the supervisor or site representative.
  5. Stop work immediately and notify the supervisor if any unsafe condition, equipment defect, or situation arises that may endanger personnel, property, product quality, or the environment.
✔ I/We confirm that the above General Site Safety Rules have been explained and understood by all workers listed in this PTW.
